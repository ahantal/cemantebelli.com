About Cem

Hello, I’m Cem.

I believe that teaching is not simply about transferring information. It is about understanding how a person learns, finding the right way to explain something, and helping that person become more confident step by step.

I graduated from Marmara University, Department of Ceramics and Glass, where I also completed my Master’s degree. I received my Pedagogical Formation Certificate from Marmara University Faculty of Education.

My professional journey has taken me through very different fields. I worked as a ceramic artist and ran my own workshop, later spent many years working in management and human resources, and eventually returned to something I had always wanted to do: teaching.

After passing the Ministry of National Education equivalency examination, I obtained my English Instructor Certificate.

For more than ten years, I have taught students of different ages through language schools and private lessons, teaching English, Turkish and Art.

Today, I especially enjoy working with people who are learning Turkish because they live in, work in, or simply want to understand Türkiye better.

And this is where my background in art becomes part of my teaching style.

I like using visuals, everyday situations, conversation, stories and creative exercises whenever they help make a subject easier to understand and remember.

Because every student is different.

Some people learn by speaking. Some need to see things. Some learn through repetition, while others need to understand the logic behind what they are learning.

My job is not to make you fit the lesson.
The lesson should fit you.
