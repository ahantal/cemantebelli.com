About Cem

Cem Antebelli

A way of teaching where language, education and art meet.

For more than 10 years, Cem Antebelli has taught Turkish, English and art to students of different ages.

Alongside his experience teaching in language schools, he has given one-to-one private lessons for many years.

He teaches Turkish especially to foreigners who live or work in Türkiye, or who want to understand the country better.

Experience

Alongside language teaching, Cem’s background includes art, ceramics, studio work, management and human resources.

He worked as a ceramic artist and ran his own studio.

This experience from very different fields is part of the way he teaches today.

Teaching Approach

Every student is different.

One person learns more easily by seeing, another by speaking or by doing.

So instead of applying one method to everyone, Cem takes the time to get to know each student and to build a way of working that suits them.

Pictures, everyday situations, conversation, stories and creative exercises can all be part of this approach.
