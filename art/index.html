Art & Drawing Lessons

Art is not only about learning technique.

It is also a way of learning to look, to see, to observe, and to express what you see in your own way.

Cem holds a bachelor’s and a master’s degree from the Department of Ceramics and Glass at Marmara University, and he brings his art education and his own creative experience into one-to-one lessons.

Lessons are open to students of all ages.

A standard lesson is 50 minutes.

Lessons take place online or in person in Mersin.

  • All ages
  • One-to-one
  • 50-minute lessons
  • Online or in person in Mersin

In the Lessons

You can work on:

  • Drawing skills
  • Different drawing and painting techniques
  • Observation
  • Visual thinking
  • Creativity
  • Hands-on practice

What you work on is shaped around your age, experience and interests.
