Turkish Lessons for Foreigners

Living in Türkiye and truly being part of life here are two very different things.

Speaking Turkish does more than make everyday life easier. It helps you build more natural relationships with people and understand the culture around you more closely.

Cem’s Turkish lessons are designed especially for foreigners who live or work in Türkiye, or who want a stronger connection with the country.

Lessons are open to all levels, from beginner to advanced.

You can join from anywhere in the world and learn from a teacher who lives in Türkiye.

How Lessons Work

Lessons are one-to-one and prepared around your needs.

A standard lesson is 50 minutes.

Lessons take place online, wherever you are. In-person lessons are also available in Mersin.

Depending on your level, lessons can include:

  • Everyday Turkish
  • Speaking practice
  • Vocabulary
  • Grammar
  • Listening and comprehension
  • Reading and writing
  • Situations you meet in daily life
  • Conversations and examples that help you understand social and cultural life in Türkiye

Turkish from Real Life

Ordering in a restaurant, taking a taxi, shopping, talking to your neighbour or chatting with friends...

Language lives in everyday life.

That is why lessons don’t just follow the book and the grammar. They also focus on how Turkish is really used, so you can speak with confidence and follow everyday conversations.

A Way of Learning That Suits You

Lessons can use pictures, conversation, stories, examples from everyday life and creative activities.

The programme is shaped around your level, the way you learn and your reasons for learning Turkish.

When it helps, lessons can be taught in a mix of English and Turkish.
