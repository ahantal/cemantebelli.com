Learn Turkish from Türkiye, wherever you are.

Whether you are already living in Türkiye, planning to move here, or simply interested in learning Turkish, you can join Cem’s lessons from anywhere in the world.

Cem lives in Mersin, Türkiye, so you learn authentic, everyday Turkish from a teacher who uses it every day, with a direct connection to life and culture here.

Don’t Just Learn Turkish. Use It.

Learning a language is more than memorizing words and grammar rules.

The real goal is to understand it in everyday life, to speak it and to express yourself.

Cem’s Turkish lessons use everyday conversations, real-life situations, pictures, stories and creative activities.

Each lesson is shaped around your level, the way you learn, your needs and your goals.

Turkish for Foreigners Living in Türkiye

If you live or work in Türkiye, learning Turkish can make a real difference to your everyday life.

At the market, in a restaurant, in a taxi, with your neighbours, your friends or at work: Turkish helps you express yourself more easily, understand what is going on around you and build a stronger connection with the people you live among.

Lessons are open to all levels and shaped around what you need day to day.

Lessons are online, so you can join from wherever you are. If you are in Mersin, you can also meet Cem in person.

Not everyone learns the same way.

Some people learn best by speaking, some by seeing, some by writing and others by doing.

That is why the content and style of each lesson are adapted to your level and the way you learn.

The aim is not just to get through a syllabus. It is for you to actually start using Turkish.

About Cem Antebelli

For more than 10 years, Cem Antebelli has taught Turkish, English and art to students of different ages, in language schools and through private lessons.

He works especially with foreigners who live or work in Türkiye, or who want to understand the country better.

His lessons draw on pictures, everyday life, conversation, stories and creative activities.

He believes every student learns differently, so he adapts his lessons to each student’s level, needs and goals.
