Turkish Lessons

Learn the Turkish you can actually use.

Whether you are new to Türkiye or have been living here for years, Turkish becomes much easier when you can use it in real-life situations.

Talking with your neighbours. Ordering at a café. Shopping, everyday tasks, communicating at work, socializing. We start from where you are and build from there.

About Cem

Hello, I’m Cem.

I believe that teaching is not simply about transferring information. It is about understanding how a person learns, finding the right way to explain something, and helping that person become more confident step by step.

My job is not to make you fit the lesson.
The lesson should fit you.
